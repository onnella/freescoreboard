Quick tips

  • Tap a team's box to add points. Tap the minus button to take points away.
  • Tap a team's name to rename it. Hold a box to change its color or drag it to reorder.
  • Drag a box off the left or right edge to remove a team.
  • Open the … menu to change points per tap or save the scores. Swipe left on the scoreboard to see saved scores.
  • Use the timer button next to Add Team to set a countdown that beeps at zero.
