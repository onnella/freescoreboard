Privacy Policy

Free Scoreboard (the "app") · Last updated: October 10, 2026

The short version

Free Scoreboard does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no network connection.

What stays on your device

The team names, colors and scores you enter, and the scoreboards you choose to save, are stored only in the app's private storage on your own device. We never see them and they are never sent anywhere.

Deleting the app deletes this data. You can also delete individual saved scores inside the app.

What we don't do

  • We do not collect personal information of any kind.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
  • We do not use third-party services or SDKs.
  • We do not share information with anyone, because we don't have any.

Children

The app doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this page with an updated date.
